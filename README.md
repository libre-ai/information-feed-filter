# Libre AI Information Feed Filter

## Intended use

Help a person turn a set of information subscriptions into an inspectable selection. Each retained item should keep its source and explain which explicit rule selected it, rather than present an unexplained recommendation.

## Existing candidates and limits

Earlier sources include normalization, rule evaluation and destination-policy components. Their presence does not establish a complete feed reader. This documentary candidate admits no fetcher, persistent subscription service, working interface or end-to-end export.

## Proposed contracts

A subscription identifies a source and the conditions for retrieving it. A normalized item retains provenance and relevant dates. A selection record identifies the rule and its inputs. An export states what was selected and what could not be processed. These remain proposals until the relevant canonical contracts are admitted by Contracts.

## Activation criteria

Qualify a complete import, retrieval, selection and export journey with real reviewed source formats. Test malformed feeds, unavailable sources, duplicate entries and explicit rule explanations. Verify destination restrictions at the time of each request, including redirects, and distinguish intentional source retrieval from transmission of a person’s private subscription data. Review rights, persistence and exact consumers before declaring the complete product journey available.

The complete journey described above is a criterion for activating the product. A candidate module may be qualified and admitted separately for its own explicit scope, with its own consumer and evidence. Neither that module admission nor the documentary existence of this repository requires a complete Missions journey or completion of other future products.
